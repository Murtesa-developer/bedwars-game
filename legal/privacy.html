BedWars - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Developer contact: murtesa.developer@proton.me

Summary

BedWars is an offline game. It does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information.

Information We Collect

None. The game does not ask you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email, location or any other personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

Data Stored On Your Device

The game saves only your game settings (mouse sensitivity, volume, fullscreen option and key bindings) in a small local file named settings.cfg in the game's own user data folder on your PC. This file stays on your device and is never uploaded. It is removed when you uninstall the game.

Internet And Third Parties

BedWars does not connect to the internet and does not use any third-party services. Because no data leaves your device, no data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

The game runs as a desktop app on Windows 10 and Windows 11. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files outside its own data folder.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. It has cartoon-style block combat with no gore.

Microsoft Store

Downloading the game through the Microsoft Store is covered by Microsoft's own privacy statement. Microsoft, not the developer, handles purchase and download information.

Changes To This Policy

If the game ever adds online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: murtesa.developer@proton.me
